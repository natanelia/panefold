# Code of conduct

Panefold welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and
perspectives. We commit to a collaborative environment where people can participate without
harassment, intimidation, or exclusion.

## Expected conduct

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive in technical disagreement.
- Critique ideas and changes, never a person's identity, ability, or motives.
- Make space for questions and differing experience levels.
- Respect privacy, attribution, boundaries, and requests to stop an interaction.
- Own mistakes, repair harm where possible, and accept moderator decisions.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption,
and sustained personal attacks are unacceptable. This applies in repository spaces and in related
public or private interactions where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a confidential conduct report, use the dedicated
[private contact request](https://github.com/natanelia/panefold/issues/new?template=private_contact.yml)
and select **Conduct report**. Do not include report details in that public request. Project
maintainers will establish a private channel and limit disclosure to what is needed to investigate,
hear affected people, avoid conflicts of interest, and choose a proportionate response. Responses
may range from a private warning through temporary restrictions to permanent removal from project
spaces.

Good-faith reports will not be retaliated against. Knowingly false or weaponized reports may
themselves violate this policy. For vulnerabilities rather than conduct concerns, follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy is a concise project adaptation inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) and its
community-care principles.
